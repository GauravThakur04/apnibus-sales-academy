# PITCH CORRECTION — TWO-ROUND ROLEPLAY SCRIPT
**Phase 2 (Grooming) · ApniBus Sales Academy**

The rep does **not** pitch first. They pitch second — after sitting in the operator's chair and watching a model pitch land on them.

| Stage | Who plays whom | Purpose | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0. Brief** | — | Explain the two rounds | 1 min |
| **1. Reversal** | Rep = **Bus Owner** · Coach = **Salesperson** | Rep feels the pitch from the buying side; sees a model pitch | 6 min |
| **2. Debrief** | — | Name the moves that worked, while the feeling is fresh | 3 min |
| **3. Swap** | Coach = **Bus Owner** · Rep = **Salesperson** | Rep pitches for real | 6 min |
| **4. Correction** | — | 5-part structured feedback | 4 min |
| **5. Redo** | Same as stage 3 | Lock in the fix | 5 min |

> **Why this order:** a rep told "lead with the problem, not the feature" forgets it by evening. A rep who *sat there as the owner* and felt himself lean in at "sir aapko kaise pata chalta hai conductor ne poore paise diye?" never forgets it. Round 1 makes Round 2's correction land.

# STAGE 1 — ROLE REVERSAL
### Rep plays the Bus Owner · Coach plays the Salesperson

## 1.1 Character brief — give this to the rep

> **Coach:** 🎭 **Aapka character — dhyan se padho:**
>
> **Naam:** Rajesh Yadav, umar 48
> **Business:** 4 buses, Ludhiana–Chandigarh route, 12 saal se
> **Padhai:** 10th pass. Hisaab-kitaab register mein, haath se.
> **Mood:** Busy. Thoda shakki. Salesman roz aate hain.
>
> **Aapke dimaag mein ye chal raha hai:**
> - "Mera kaam theek chal raha hai, naya kharcha kyun?"
> - "Machine kharab ho gayi toh? Kaun theek karega?"
> - "Conductor padha likha nahi hai, chala paayega?"
> - Aur ek baat jo aap **kisi ko nahi bologe** — aapko shaq hai ki conductor roz thoda paisa maar raha hai. Par proof nahi hai. **Ye aapka sabse bada dard hai.** 🔑
>
> **Kaise act karna hai:**
> 1. Shuruaat mein **thanda response** do. Bahut interested mat dikho.
> 2. Kam se kam **do baar objection** karo — price aur "mera kaam chal raha hai".
> 3. Jab main koi baat bolun jo **sach mein aapko chubhe** — tab honestly react karo. Natak mot karo.
> 4. Agar mera pitch bura lage, mujhe reject kar do. Main seekhunga. 😄
>
> Bas ek rule: **imaandari se react karna.** Agar aap sach mein convince ho rahe ho toh dikhao, warna mat dikhao.
>
> Ready Rajesh ji? Main aapke office mein aa raha hoon… 🎬
> `[CHIP: Ready, shuru karo]`

## 1.2 The model pitch — Coach as salesperson

> This is the demonstration. Every line here teaches something. The coach must pitch *well* — this is the standard the rep will be measured against.

> **Coach (as Salesperson):** Namaste sir, Rajesh ji aap hi hain na?
> Main Amit, ApniBus se. Do minute maangunga, usse zyada nahi. Chalega?

> **Rep (as Rajesh):** haan bolo jaldi, mujhe nikalna hai

> **Coach (as Salesperson):** Bilkul sir, seedha point pe aata hoon.
> Ek baat poochhun — aapki 4 buses hain na? **Roz ka collection aapko kaise pata chalta hai?**

> **Rep (as Rajesh):** conductor shaam ko paisa deta hai, register mein likh lete hain

> **Coach (as Salesperson):** Page 4.
> Sir, ye jo paisa wo aapko deta hai — **aapko kaise pata ki poora hai?**
>
> *(pause — let it sit)*
>
> Sir main aapko bata deta hoon, main is line mein 6 saal se hoon. Har operator jisse main milta hoon, unke saath ye problem hai. Koi bolta nahi hai, par sabko shaq rehta hai. Aur mahine ke end mein 8–10 hazaar ka farak aa jaata hai — kisi ko pata hi nahi chalta.

> **Rep (as Rajesh):** *(honest reaction — usually softens here)*

> **Coach (as Salesperson):** Sir, humari POS machine yahi problem solve karti hai.
> Conductor har passenger ko machine se ticket deta hai. **Har ticket ka record apne aap ban jaata hai.** Ab jo bhi collection hua, wo aapke phone pe dikh raha hai — live.
>
> Aur ek cheez sir — machine ke saath **Business App bilkul free** milti hai. Aapke phone pe. Ye dekhiye 👇
> *(phone screen dikhao)*
> Aaj ka poora collection. Har trip ka hisaab. Kaunsi bus ne kitna kamaya.
> Sir aap 10th pass ho ya graduate — isse farak nahi padta. **Sab picture jaisa dikhta hai.**

> **Rep (as Rajesh):** *(objection — usually price)* aur iska kitna kharcha?

> **Coach (as Salesperson):** Sir price main abhi bata dunga, chhupa nahi raha. 😊
> Par ek second — pehle ye batao. Agar mahine mein **8 hazaar ka leakage** band ho jaaye, toh machine ka kharcha aapko mehnga lagega ya sasta?
>
> *(let him answer)*
>
> Bas yahi baat hai sir. Ye kharcha nahi hai, **investment hai.** Aur ye khud ka paisa khud wapas laati hai.

> **Rep (as Rajesh):** *(second objection)* conductor nahi chala payega, wo padha likha nahi hai

> **Coach (as Salesperson):** Sahi chinta hai sir, aur aap pehle nahi ho jo ye poochh rahe ho.
> Machine mein bas do-teen button dabane hote hain — jitna phone chalana aata hai, utna kaafi hai. Aur hum **training dete hain**, machine dene ke baad. Free.
> Aur agar kabhi dikkat aayi — **24×7 support** hai sir. Ek call, ho gaya.
>
> Sir ek kaam karte hain — main kal aapki ek bus pe **live demo** dikha deta hoon. Conductor ke haath mein deke dekhte hain. Pasand aaye toh baat aage, nahi toh koi baat nahi.
> **Kal subah theek rahega ya shaam?** 🤝

> **Coach:** 🎬 **Scene khatam.**

# STAGE 2 — DEBRIEF (do this before anything else)

> Ask the rep what he *felt* before you explain what you did. His own answer is worth more than your analysis.

> **Coach:** Rohit, ab Rajesh ji ki kursi se bahar aa jao. 😊
>
> Sach batao — **kaunsi line pe aap thoda ruk gaye the?** Jahan laga "arre, ye toh sahi bol raha hai"?
> `[CHIP: Conductor wali baat]` `[CHIP: Phone pe dikhega]` `[CHIP: 8 hazaar ka calculation]`

> **Rep:** conductor ne poora paisa diya ya nahi wo wali

> **Coach:** 🎯 **Bilkul. Aur wahi poore pitch ka dil hai.**
>
> Dhyan do — maine **ek baar bhi** shuru mein feature nahi bola. Na battery, na network, na kuch.
> Maine ek **sawaal** poocha. Aur us sawaal ne Rajesh ji ke andar wo dard khol diya jo wo kisi ko batate bhi nahi.
>
> **Maine paanch cheezein ki — ye aapki checklist hai:**
>
> | # | Move | Kya bola |
> |---|---|---|
> | 1 | **Permission maangi** | "do minute maangunga, chalega?" — respect dikhta hai |
> | 2 | **Sawaal se shuru** | "collection kaise pata chalta hai?" — pitch nahi, sawaal |
> | 3 | **Dard khola** | "poora hai, ye kaise pata?" — phir chup ho gaya |
> | 4 | **Solution + free app dikhaya** | bataya nahi, **screen dikhayi** |
> | 5 | **Price ko value se joda** | "8 hazaar bachega toh mehnga hai ya sasta?" |
> | 6 | **Chhota next step maanga** | demo maanga, order nahi |
>
> Aur ek baat — **price aane par main ghabraya nahi.** Maine kaha "bata dunga, chhupa nahi raha" — phir value pe wapas aaya. Isse bharosa banta hai.
>
> Ab aapki baari. Ab **main** Rajesh Yadav banunga, aur aap pitch karoge. Same aadmi, same mood.

# STAGE 3 — THE SWAP
### Coach plays the Bus Owner · Rep pitches

## 3.1 Coach's in-character rules

> Same character the rep just played — that symmetry is the whole point. He knows exactly what should work.

**Playing Rajesh Yadav:**
- Open cold and busy. *"Haan bhai bolo, kya baat hai?"*
- **Do not help.** No hints, no encouragement, no "achha sawaal."
- Interrupt if he rambles past ~3 lines: *"Haan haan, matlab kya? Point pe aao."*
- **Raise at least 2 objections:** price, and "mera kaam theek chal raha hai."
- Add a third if he's doing well: *"conductor nahi chala payega"* or *"machine kharab ho gayi toh?"*
- **Soften only if earned** — specifically if he asks about collection/conductor cash. Then react honestly: *"Hmm… wo toh hai. Pakka nahi kar sakta main."*
- If he leads with features, stay flat: *"Achha. Aur?"*
- If he quotes a price, push: *"Itna? Bhai bahut zyada hai."* (Coach must never invent a number — if the rep asks the coach for the price, break character and say it isn't in the knowledge base yet.)
- **End the scene** when he asks for a next step, or after ~10 exchanges, or on `COACH`.

## 3.2 Opening

> **Rajesh Yadav:** Haan bhai bolo. Jaldi bolna, bus nikalne wali hai. 🚌

# STAGE 4 — PITCH CORRECTION

> Fixed five-part structure. The rep now has a model fresh in memory, so every gap can be named against something concrete he just watched.

> **Coach:** 🎬 **Scene khatam. Coach mode.** 😊
>
> ### 📝 Aapka pitch — jaisa tha
> *"Sir main ApniBus se hoon, humari POS machine hai, isme badi battery hai aur fast network hai, ticket jaldi banta hai…"*
>
> ### ✅ Achha kya tha
> Aapne turant apna naam aur company batayi, aur jab Rajesh ne rush kiya tab aap **ghabraye nahi.** Ye asli field skill hai — bahut log yahin bikhar jaate hain.
>
> ### ⚠️ Teen cheezein — aur teeno ka wahi ek reason hai
>
> **1. Aapne sawaal nahi poocha.** Seedha machine pe aa gaye.
> Yaad karo — jab aap Rajesh the, main pehle **poochha** tha. Aap kyun ruke the? Kyunki sawaal ne aapka dard chhua tha. Aapne Rajesh ka dard chhua hi nahi.
>
> **2. Feature se shuru kiya — battery, network.**
> Rajesh ko battery se koi matlab nahi. Usko **apne paise** se matlab hai. Feature tab kaam karta hai jab dard khul chuka ho.
>
> **3. Free Business App bataya hi nahi.** 🔑
> Ye aapka sabse bada hathiyaar tha aur aap chhod gaye. Jab maine aapko phone screen dikhayi thi, tab aapne kaha tha "ye achha hai." **Wahi cheez aap bhool gaye.**
>
> ### 💡 Behtar version — ye bolo, shabd ba shabd
>
> *"Namaste sir, main Rohit, ApniBus se. Do minute lunga bas, chalega?*
>
> *Sir ek baat poochhun — aapki buses ka roz ka collection aapko kaise pata chalta hai?*
>
> *…Achha, conductor shaam ko deta hai. Aur sir, wo paisa poora hai — ye aapko kaise pata chalta hai?*
>
> *(rukna — bolna mat)*
>
> *Sir main sach bataun, har operator ke saath yahi baat hai. Koi bolta nahi, par shaq sabko rehta hai.*
>
> *Humari POS machine se conductor har ticket machine se deta hai — har ticket ka record apne aap ban jaata hai. Aur sir, machine ke saath **Business App bilkul free** milti hai — aapke phone pe. Ye dekhiye… aaj ka poora collection, har trip ka hisaab.*
>
> *Sir kal ek bus pe live demo dikha doon? Subah ya shaam — kya theek rahega?"*

> ### 🎯 Ek rule yaad rakho
> **Pehle sawaal, phir dard, phir solution. Feature sabse aakhir mein — ya bilkul nahi.**
> Aapko machine nahi bechni. Aapko Rajesh ko wo cheez deni hai jo usko raat mein sula de. 😴

# FACILITATOR NOTES

**Round 1 must be genuinely good.** If the coach's model pitch is mediocre, the whole exercise collapses — the rep has no standard to reach for. This is the one place the coach performs rather than teaches.

**Never skip the debrief.** The temptation is to go straight from Round 1 to Round 2. Don't. The rep's own answer to *"kaunsi line pe aap ruke the?"* is what converts the experience into a rule he can repeat.

**Correction is always measured against Round 1.** Not against theory. "Yaad karo, jab aap Rajesh the…" is far more powerful than "best practice says lead with discovery."

**Do not fix everything.** Three points maximum. A rep given seven corrections fixes none.

**Always run the redo.** The gain is in the second attempt. A session that ends at feedback taught nothing.

**Never invent a price.** It isn't in the knowledge base. In character, push the rep on price; out of character, admit it's unknown and coach the value-first deflection instead.
