    📋 Attendance Policy

    Select your Job Profile to view Attendance Policy & Quiz:

    Freelancer / ISA (Independent Sales Executive)

    No minimum daily visit requirements or fixed working hours. Simply register leads and meetings in the Commando App to track deals. No daily attendance logging is required.

    FSE (Field Sales Executive)

    Attendance is calculated automatically based on unique operator visits logged daily in the Commando App:

    Unique Visits Logged Attendance Status
    0 or 1 visit ❌ ABSENT (0 या 1 उपस्थिति: अनुपस्थित)
    2 or 3 visits ⚠️ HALF DAY (2 या 3 उपस्थिति: आधा दिन)
    4 or more visits ✅ FULL DAY (4 या अधिक उपस्थिति: पूरा दिन)
    🔥 CRITICAL RULE: IF YOU CLOSE AT LEAST ONE SALE (POS ACTIVATION), YOU WILL BE MARKED PRESENT (FULL DAY) REGARDLESS OF THE NUMBER OF VISITS!

    ✏️ Attendance Question 1/2:

    English: I have visited only 3 operators. My FTD Attendance will be?
    Hindi: प्रश्न: मैंने केवल 3 ऑपरेटर विज़िट किए हैं। मेरी FTD उपस्थिति क्या होगी?
    Hinglish: Question: Maine sirf 3 operators visit kiye hain. Meri FTD Attendance kya hogi?

    ✏️ Attendance Question 2/2:

    English: I have visited 3 operators today and have closed 1 sale. My attendance will be?
    Hindi: प्रश्न: मैंने आज 3 ऑपरेटर विज़िट किए हैं और 1 बिक्री (Sale) क्लोज़ की है। मेरी उपस्थिति क्या होगी?
    Hinglish: Question: Maine aaj 3 operators visit kiye hain aur 1 sale close ki hai. Meri attendance kya hogi?
